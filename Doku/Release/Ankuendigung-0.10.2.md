## Ko-fi-Beitrag (Englisch, Titel + Text, Bild: `docs/img/hero.jpg` hochladen)

**Titel:** FICSIT Tower Defense 0.10.2 – meet the Pioneer

**Text:**

The biggest update so far is live on ficsit.app: **you are no longer just the builder.**

**The Pioneer** fights alongside your turrets. Every wave you earn XP for your share of the damage, your kills, assists and repairs. Each rank gives a Talent Point for one of four roles:

- **Marksman** – headshots, fast reload, piercing and explosive rounds
- **Commander** – mark enemies, rally your turrets, overdrive everything around you
- **Engineer** – field repair beam, overdrive button, ammo resupply, bulwark
- **Hunter** – hits harder on elites and bosses, guaranteed loot, execution

Also new: Coupons and research now use the game's own items (Coupons and Hard Drives), a **Game Mode** menu when you start a new game, and a round of fixes, including a crash on quick reloads.

It is still an alpha, so every bug report helps: Core Menu → *Report bug / idea*.

📖 Player guide: https://spancken.github.io/FicsitTD/

**FICSIT Tower Defense 0.10.2 (alpha)** – factory-fed tower defense for Satisfactory 1.2, single-player.
