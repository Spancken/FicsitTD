**Text:**

Hi pioneers! A week ago I posted **FICSIT Tower Defense**, a single-player mod where your factory has to produce the ammo for your turrets. Thanks for all the feedback – 0.13 is out, and it is the biggest update so far.

**Flyers.** From wave 43 a **flock of birds** heads straight for the Defense Core, over every wall. From wave 45 **Mantas** join them: they circle the Core and spit acid at it, and they can take a lot of hits. Ground turrets can't touch them – you need the **Sky Tower** and your own hand weapons.

**A new start.** You get 40 Coupons for your first turrets. ADA asks for the HUB first, after wave 1 for the game's own **AWESOME Shop** (quotas, gear, core repairs, Hard Drives), after wave 2 for the MAM.

**Core access rules.** If your buildings seal off the Core, the wave doesn't start – the blocking spot is marked on the map, and the hologram turns red before you close the last path. No factory buildings within 60 m of the Core ("unstable ground"), turrets and logistics are fine.

**Research.** The MAM tree is untangled (six entry points in Industry, turrets from wave 3/5), and there is a new **Power tree**: coal, oil and – after wave 50 – nuclear, where better fuels give every generator more MW.

**Harder second half.** Enemies get tougher much faster from wave 31, so the late waves stay dangerous. Endless mode stays as it was.

Plus enemies that find their way through wall mazes, walk around machines and turrets, a Hard Drive trade-in, an oil finder at the start, and a long list of fixes.

It's still an **alpha** – feedback is very welcome, in-game via Core Menu → *Report bug / idea*.

Full disclosure: I designed, tested and balanced it myself, but a lot of the code was written with AI help (Claude) – the mod page says so too.

Download (Satisfactory Mod Manager): https://ficsit.app/mod/FicsitTD
Player guide: https://spancken.github.io/FicsitTD/
