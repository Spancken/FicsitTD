**Text:**

Hi pioneers! **FICSIT Tower Defense** is a single-player mod that turns Satisfactory into a tower defense where **your factory makes the ammo**: rebar, rifle ammo, nobelisks and fuel run by belt and pipe into your turrets, and waves of native fauna try to reach the Defense Core in your start biome. 0.13 is out – the biggest update so far.

**New to the mod?** Right after landing you pick how to play: an **Operation** of 50 waves with bosses, **Endless waves** that never stop, or **Free play with defense** (regular Satisfactory plus turrets, fights only when you want them). You fight alongside your turrets and earn Pioneer ranks and talents. Losing never deletes your factory.

**Played it before? What's new in 0.13:**

- **Flyers.** From wave 43 a **flock of birds** heads straight for the Core, over every wall. From wave 45 **Mantas** join them: they circle the Core and spit acid, and they take a lot of hits. Ground turrets can't touch them – you need the **Sky Tower** and your own hand weapons. The flying crabs are gone.
- **A new start.** 40 Coupons for your first turrets. ADA asks for the HUB first, after wave 1 for the game's own **AWESOME Shop** (quotas, gear, core repairs, Hard Drives), after wave 2 for the MAM.
- **Core access rules.** If your buildings seal off the Core, the wave doesn't start – the hologram turns red before you close the last path. No factory buildings within 60 m of the Core; turrets and logistics are fine. Enemies now find their way through wall mazes.
- **Research.** The MAM tree is untangled, and there is a new **Power tree**: coal, oil and – after wave 50 – nuclear, where better fuels give every generator more MW.
- **Harder second half.** Enemies get tougher much faster from wave 31. Endless mode stays as it was.

Your 0.11 saves load (Talent Points are reset once).

It's still an **alpha** – feedback is very welcome, in-game via Core Menu → *Report bug / idea*.

Full disclosure: I designed, tested and balanced it myself, but a lot of the code was written with AI help (Claude) – the mod page says so too.
