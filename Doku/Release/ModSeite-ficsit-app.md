# FICSIT Tower Defense

**Your factory makes the ammo. The turrets protect the factory.**

### 📖 [Player guide](https://spancken.github.io/FicsitTD/) · 🐞 [Report a bug or idea](https://github.com/Spancken/FicsitTD/issues/new) · ⚠️ [Known issues](https://spancken.github.io/FicsitTD/#issues)
*The player guide (English/German) explains game modes, turrets, ammo chains, research and the Pioneer. Bugs and ideas can also be reported right in the game: Core Menu → **Report bug / idea**.*

> 🧪 **Alpha – playtesters wanted!** FICSIT Tower Defense is playable from wave 1 to 50, but balance and some features are still rough, and not every feature has been tested in a full run yet. Please play it, break it and tell me what feels wrong or boring: **Core Menu → Report bug / idea**, or https://github.com/Spancken/FicsitTD/issues. Every report helps shape the next version.

FICSIT Tower Defense turns Satisfactory into a tower defense that lives off your factory. A FICSIT Defense Core stands in your start biome, and waves of native fauna attack it from up to three fronts. Every turret fires **real ammunition from your own production lines**, delivered by conveyor belt or pipe. Fifty waves lead you from simple iron rebar to plasma flames.

## 🆕 Back for 0.13? What's new
The biggest update so far. **Your 0.11 saves load**: Talent Points are reset once to the new boss points, and the Core no longer sells – build the AWESOME Shop after wave 1 instead.

- **Untangled research:** Industry has six entry points without a forced chain; Bolt and Tesla from wave 3, Rifle Turret from wave 5.
- **New Power tree:** coal, oil and (after wave 50) nuclear power, nothing locked out. Better fuels give every generator more output – Compacted Coal 100 MW, Petroleum Coke 125 MW, Turbofuel 325 MW, Rocket Fuel 400 MW. At the start of an operation the chat tells you where the nearest crude oil is.
- **Harder second half:** Normal, Hard and Nightmare have more enemy health that grows much faster from wave 31. Endless waves stay as before.

## 👋 New here?
- **Your factory feeds the defense.** Turrets fire real ammunition – rebar, rifle ammo, nobelisks, fuel – that your own production lines deliver by belt or pipe. No ammo, no defense.
- **Three ways to play**, picked right after landing: an **Operation** of 50 waves with bosses and phase reports, **Endless waves** that never stop (how far can you get?), or **Free play with defense** – regular Satisfactory with turrets and drilling at resource nodes when *you* want a fight.
- **You fight too:** the Pioneer earns ranks and Talent Points for four roles (below).
- **Losing never deletes your factory.** Start with the Quick start further down; the [player guide](https://spancken.github.io/FicsitTD/) explains everything.

### The Pioneer

You are no longer just the builder. Fight next to your turrets and earn **Pioneer ranks** for your share of the damage, kills, assists and repairs. Every cleared boss wave gives two Talent Points (10 in an operation, up to 15 with Endless waves and drilling) for one of **four roles**, five abilities each; ranks add health and speed:

- **Marksman** – headshots, fast reload, piercing shots, ammo saving, explosive rounds
- **Commander** – mark enemies for +15 % turret damage, marks jump on, **rally order** (nearby turrets fire at your target), overdrive for all turrets around you
- **Engineer** – **field repair** with Iron Plates, **overdrive button** in the turret window (+50 % clock for 10 s), ammo resupply from your inventory, bulwark (one building per wave survives at half health)
- **Hunter** – ×1.5 against elites and bosses, guaranteed loot, weak spots, resistances on the boss bar, execution

Take two roles fully or three halfway. Your strongest role shows as a badge in the HUD, and repairs, rally orders and overdrive are visible on the turrets.

## Quick start
1. Back up your saves. Start a **new game**. Right after landing, a window asks how you want to play: **Operation**, **Endless waves**, **Free play with defense** or **Off**, plus the difficulty. Existing worlds without a Defense Core stay regular Satisfactory.
2. After landing, the mod builds a platform with the **Defense Core** near your start. ADA briefs you and asks for the HUB first (power and respawn point).
3. Build miners, smelters and constructors, then a **Bolt Turret**. Feed it rebar by conveyor and connect power.
4. Press **E** at the Core and hit **Start wave** when you are ready. There is no timer.

## What you get
- **Three game modes:** Operation (50 waves), Endless waves (timed, never ends, best result per difficulty) and Free play with defense (regular Satisfactory plus turrets and an optional drilling challenge).
- **An operation of 50 waves** in five phases with a boss every tenth wave, elites and new enemy types: Hogs, Stingers, forest, desert and water Spitters, Nuclear Hogs, Johnny – and from wave 43 birds and Mantas from the sky. Every core site has its own fixed fronts, so a site always plays the same.
- **5 turret lines with real ammo chains:** Bolt Turret (rebar → piercing → shrapnel/stun → explosive), Rifle Turret (rifle ammo → homing/armor-piercing → incendiary → turbo), Nobelisk Launcher (nobelisk → gas/pulse → cluster), Tesla Coil (power, chain lightning or overload) and Flamethrower (fuel → turbofuel → rocket fuel → ionized fuel). Late game: Sky Tower (against birds from wave 43 and Mantas from wave 45) and Singularity Launcher (Endless mode, after wave 50).
- **Ammo that lasts:** one item fires several shots (basic 4, advanced 3, special 2). The turret window shows the consumption per minute and how many turrets a Mk.1 belt feeds.
- **Turret levels 1–5** bought with Coupons in the turret window, visible on the model. Level 5 unlocks a master ability per turret. Pick a **doctrine** for your main line.
- **Seven combos** between turrets and buildings: Lightning Rod, Heat Crack, Deflagration, Oil Fire, Chokepoint, Rally Point, Conductor.
- **Defensive buildings:** walls and gate, lure fire, oil slick field, electric fence, radio mast (radar and front warnings), shield generator, jammer projector, drone pad with repair or combat drones.
- **Quotas instead of free building:** production and defense buildings are limited per type; FICSIT Coupons from the waves (the game's Coupon item in your inventory) buy more in the game's own AWESOME Shop, which you build after wave 1 (category FICSIT Defense, every purchase raises the next price). Belts, lifts, pipes and power lines stay free; splitters, mergers and storage count as buildings. Foundations, walls and base game shop items are not available during an operation.
- **MAM research** with five trees (Industry, Power, Ballistics, Elemental, Fortress), branches where you choose one side per operation, paid with **Hard Drives** from waves (the game's own Hard Drive). HUB milestones and blueprints are locked during an operation.
- **Your own share counts:** hand weapons use the turret ammo values and grow with the waves; the wave report shows your damage, share, assists and Pioneer XP.
- **Losing never deletes your factory.** After a defeat you can retry the wave or end the operation; every tenth wave closes a phase with a short report and a bonus pick, and the end of a run shows a final report and your best result per core site and difficulty. The mod saves before and after every wave; saving during a wave is locked.
- **English and German**, following the game language.

## Status: Alpha (0.13.0)

**Requires Satisfactory 1.2.5.1** (build 520140). Older versions of the mod (0.10.2 and before) crash on this game version.
- Steam and Epic, Windows. **Single-player only**; multiplayer and dedicated servers are not supported.
- Balance is work in progress; the target length is 50 waves.
- Hard and Nightmare are harder since 0.13 and not fully balanced yet.
- **Known issues** (occasional stuck enemies, Tesla power spikes, retry wave untested and more): https://spancken.github.io/FicsitTD/#issues
